补上的答案，已经是一个决策。

The filled answer is already a decision.

世界模型交回一幅没有洞的下一帧，脑电解码把丢包写成零，规划器把没测到的格子当成空地。下一模块分不出这是测到的，还是写上去的。

A world model returns a next frame with no holes. A neural decoder writes a dropped packet as zeros. A planner treats an unseen cell as free. The next module cannot tell a measurement from a write.

怎么读 · How to read this page

先按按钮，再看名词。数字是按钮背后的陈述的见证，不是另一篇文章。

  1. 走廊上的三个按钮：补成空地、只追问一次、没看见就不进。
  2. 脑电窗口：实录、非负补零、删掉一个负数。
  3. 奖励表：只看当前行，和一次回溯。
  4. 不认识的词，看下面的名词。证明在仓库的笔记里，不在这个页面上。

Press the buttons first. The numbers later are witnesses of what the buttons do, not a second article. The corridor, then the EEG window, then the reward row. Unfamiliar words are in the list below. The proofs live in the note, not on this page.

名词 · Words

补全。给没测到的地方写上一个能用的值，再把它当成测到的。乐观补全写成空地或零。悲观补全写成挡住。

Fill. Write a usable value where nothing was measured, then treat it as measured. An optimistic fill writes “free” or zero. A pessimistic fill writes “blocked.”

实测步。只用观测到的项。没看见的格子不进入。

Measured step. Use only what was observed. An unseen cell is not entered.

掩码。“这里没测到”的标记。补全会把它删掉，所以事后的缺失检查什么也找不到。

Mask. The mark that a cell was not observed. A fill deletes it, so a later missing-data check finds nothing.

平局。两条路一样长。交出去的决策留下只走已观测空地的那条。

Tie. Two paths of equal length. The shipped decision keeps the one that uses only observed free cells.

一次追问。封住第一次撞上的格子再规划。其余没看见的格子仍被写成空地，所以可能再撞。

One question. Block the first crash and plan again. The other unseen cells stay filled free, so the path can crash again.

空输入。里面什么都没有。这里直接拒绝，不返回零。

Empty input. An input with nothing in it. It is refused. It is not returned as zero.

一条走廊 · A corridor

五个格子。灰色是没观测到的。点下面三个按钮，看同一次观测交回什么。

Five cells. Gray was not observed. The three buttons are three writes on that same observation.

一段脑电 · An EEG window

类别是采样之和是否为正。补零等于减去被删掉的和。

The class is whether the sum is positive. Zero-fill subtracts the sum that was erased.

下一步动作 · The next action

陷阱表 [[10, 1], [−100, −100]]。只看当前行选 0。解 (I − dP)V = r，折扣一过 9/101，选 1。

Trap [[10, 1], [−100, −100]]. The visible row selects 0. Solving (I − dP)V = r, the action flips to 1 once the discount passes 9/101.

两种人，一幅画面 · Two people, one picture

一个人在适应灵巧手。画面如果把没看见的格子画成已经抓完，他会去练一个世界里没有的动作。一个人留在左右交替的声音里。这段声音很难听下去，所以游戏是一个可以待着的开放地方。如果因为一个不完整的脑电窗口去改这段声音，改动就不是对惊恐的读取。

One person is learning a dexterous hand. A picture that draws an unseen cell as a finished grasp trains a motion the world does not contain. Another person is staying with a left-right sound. The sound is hard to stay with, so the game is an open place where it can continue. Changing that sound because a brain window was incomplete is not a reading of fright.

10,000 个不完整窗口里，补零会改掉 2,421 次声音。会话一次都不改。2,000 张手部地图里，补全画面穿过障碍 1,439 次。引导把那种抓取画成完成的次数是 0。这不是治疗效果。

Of 10,000 incomplete windows, a zero-fill would retune 2,421 sounds. The session retunes none. Of 2,000 hand maps, the filled picture crosses an obstacle 1,439 times. The coach draws that finished grasp 0 times. This is not a treatment effect.

打开下面的声音。点击在左耳和右耳之间交替。窗口完整时，静息是每秒 2 下，动作是每秒 6 下。丢掉窗口时，速度留在原地。10,000 步里丢掉了 3,057 次；补零会在其中 712 次改速度；正在响的速度在丢包期间改变了 0 次。这不是惊恐检测，也不是治疗。

Play the sound below. Clicks alternate left and right. On a complete window, rest is 2 a second and a movement is 6. When the window is dropped, the speed stays. Across 10,000 steps, 3,057 windows were dropped. A zero-fill would have changed the speed on 712 of them. The speed already playing changed 0 times during a dropout. This is not a detector of fright, and it is not a treatment.

还没有播放。 Not playing.

画面里的手只走到已经看见的空地。灰色格子不画成已经走过。房间里的另外两个人站着，不要求你走过去。

The hand in the picture walks only on ground that was seen. A gray cell is not drawn as already crossed. The other two people stay where they are. You are not asked to go to them.

手在起点。 The hand is at the start.

可以留下的一行是：窗口是否完整，完整时的类别，手停在哪一格已经看见的空地。丢掉的窗口不写类别。没看见的格子不写成抓取。2,000 行里记下 1,428 个类别，丢包上的类别是 0，手落在没看见的格子上是 0。这不是从人身上采来的数据。

A row may keep whether the window was complete, the class if it was, and the seen free cell where the hand stopped. A dropped window stores no class. An unseen cell is not stored as a grasp. Of 2,000 rows, 1,428 classes are stored. Classes on a drop: 0. Hands on an unseen cell: 0. This is not data from a person.

房间里蓝色的人站在已经看见的格子上，所以一直在。红色的人站在没看见的格子上，只有「补全后再画」才出现。把没看见的人画进来，不是更安全的房间。

The blue person stands on a cell that was seen, so they stay. The red person stands on a cell that was not seen, and appears only if you draw the filled path. Drawing a person the camera did not see does not make the room safer.

没有新的观测，再画一次，像素不变。按「丢掉窗口，画面保持」就是这样。按「补全后再画」，没看见的格子也被涂上，帧就变了。2,000 张地图上都是如此：保持帧再画，变化次数是 0；补全帧全部不同，127,697 个没看见的格子得到了颜色。

Draw again with no new observation, and the pixels stay. “Drop the window, hold the picture” is that. “Draw the filled path” paints cells that were not seen, and the frame changes. On all 2,000 maps the held frame does not change when drawn again. The filled frame differs on every map, and 127,697 unseen cells receive a color.

声音和画面是一个时钟。丢掉窗口，两个都不动。看见的格子走完以后，声音还可以跟着完整的窗口走，画面停在最后一格。2,000 张地图、每张 16 个窗口：丢包时画面移动 0 次，速度改变 0 次；画面向前 3,665 次；路走完后仍听到的动作 6,414 次。

The sound and the picture are one clock. Drop the window and neither moves. After the seen cells run out, the sound can still follow a complete window and the picture stays on the last cell. On 2,000 maps, sixteen windows each: the frame moves 0 times on a drop, the rate changes 0 times on a drop, the picture steps 3,665 times, and a movement is still heard 6,414 times after the seen path has ended.

离房间里的人有多近，也看走的是哪张地图。只走看见的空地，和把没看见的格子当成空地，不是同一个距离。后者能走到、前者走不到的人，在这批地图上有 2,301 次。两条都能走到时，补全的那条更短 560 次。人不动，也不要求你走过去。更近不是更安全。

How near someone is depends on which map you walk. Seen free cells, and missing cells written as free, are not the same distance. The second walk reaches a person the first cannot, 2,301 times on these maps. When both arrive, the filled walk is shorter 560 times. People do not move, and you are not asked to go to them. Nearer is not safer.

左右声的速度不画进像素。按「完整窗口，静息」或「完整窗口，动作」，声音可以变，手可以不动。按「丢掉窗口」，两个都不变。同一批时钟上，像素改变 3,665 次；声音变了而画面没变，有 8,377 次。

The left-right rate is not painted into the pixels. “Complete, rest” or “Complete, movement” can change the sound while the hand stays. “Drop the window” changes neither. On the same clocks the pixels change 3,665 times, and the sound changes while the picture stays 8,377 times.

这两件事不要混在一起。第一件：机器更快，会不会看见另一个世界？同一批 2,000 张地图，一个进程算一遍，十个进程各算一段。补全后的路径走进真障碍的次数都是 1,439。手的画面不取决于哪一个核画的。第二件：传感器什么都没交回来时，库交给游戏的是什么？wave 能写出 0 帧的合法声音。SciPy 和 soundfile 把它读成 8,000 Hz 的空数组。Pillow 能造出 0×0 的图。OpenCV 在空图上数到 0。Open3D 交回 0 个点的点云。这些调用都成功，所以下一模块可以播放或渲染它们。有采样率却没有帧，不是“人处于静息”。存在却没有点，不是“手已经抓完”。八个真实采样才是类别 1。一个没有采样的列表会被拒绝。

Keep the two questions apart. First: does a faster machine see a different world? The same 2,000 maps, planned once in one process and once in ten slices, both walk into a real obstacle 1,439 times. The picture of the hand does not depend on which core drew it. Second: when the sensor returns nothing, what does the library hand to the game? wave writes a legal sound with 0 frames. SciPy and soundfile read it as an empty array at 8,000 Hz. Pillow builds a 0×0 image. OpenCV counts 0. Open3D returns a cloud of 0 points. Each call succeeds, so the next module can play or render it. A sample rate with no frames is not “the person is at rest.” A cloud that exists and has no points is not “the hand has finished.” Eight real samples are class 1. A list with no samples is refused.

一根手指 · One finger

闭合是代码 0。采样没到，不是代码 0。零缓冲把没到的采样写成 0，画面上这根手指就握上了。引导只保留上次真正到过的代码。一次都没到过，就不画成闭合。

Closed is code 0. A missing sample is not code 0. The zero buffer writes 0 for a missing sample, and the picture draws the finger closed. The coach keeps the last code that arrived. If none has arrived, the finger is not drawn closed.

2,000 只手、每只 16 个包、五根手指，共 160,000 步。零缓冲画成闭合、而引导没有测到闭合的，有 43,014 步。其中 38,623 步上一次看见的是张开，4,391 步这根手指一次采样都没到过。引导画出的闭合是 19,335，全部也在零缓冲里。没有收到 0 却画出闭合，是 0 次。五个关节按零分配，读出来是五个 0。这不是从人身上量到的角度。

Two thousand hands, sixteen packets, five fingers: 160,000 steps. The buffer draws a closure the coach did not observe on 43,014 steps. On 38,623 of them the finger was last seen open. On 4,391 the finger had never sent a sample. The coach draws 19,335 closures, and the buffer draws all of them. A closure with no received 0: 0. Five joints allocated as zeros read back as five zeros. These are not angles from a person.

有一边还没到 · One of them is late

脑电和手指角度是两路。游戏每一帧都要画。有一路还没到时，一种画法先用默认值把这一帧补上：脑电没到就当成没动，手指没到就画成已经握紧。给用户的画法是先留着上一次真正到过的样子。两边都到了，两种画法是同一幅。

The brain recording and the finger angle are two streams. A game has to draw every frame. When one of them is late, one drawing fills the frame with a default: no movement if the brain recording is late, a closed finger if the angle is late. The drawing for the user keeps the last values that actually arrived. When both have arrived, the two drawings match.

两家住户 · Two tenants

服务给每家各留一幅画面。A 来不来，只改 A 的画面，不改 B 的。反过来也一样。十六家排着来，和轮着来，每家看见的完全相同。多一家从不发货的，其余十六家不受影响。

The service keeps one picture per tenant. Whether A sends anything changes only A’s picture, never B’s, and the other way around. Sixteen tenants served one after another, or round-robin, each see the same sequence. Adding a tenant that never sends anything leaves the other sixteen unchanged.

画面几步没更新了 · How old is the picture

两边都是这一步到的，年龄是 0。有一边晚了，画面留着，年龄涨一步。中间三步都没到，画面不动，年龄涨三步。猜测永远说自己是刚到的，留下的那一版不会这么说。

When both streams arrived this step, the age is 0. When one is late, the picture stays and grows a step older. Through three steps with nothing arriving, the picture does not move and grows three steps older. A guess always claims to be new. The held picture never makes that claim.

蒙对了 · Right by luck

按“来一步”。真实、猜、留三块各显示这一步。晚到的步里，猜对和留对各记一次。多按几次，留对长得更快，因为手指晚了，留着角度比总说握紧蒙对更多。这是示意，浏览器现画的随机数，能重算的数在笔记里。

Press “one step.” Truth, the guess, and the held picture each show this step. On a late step, a match for either side is counted. Press several times and the held count grows faster: when the finger is late, keeping the angle beats always saying closed. This demo draws fresh random numbers in the browser. The recomputable counts are in the note.

断完第几步追上 · Catch up after a burst

按“断三步”，再按“来一步”。两边都到的第一步，留和真实对上，这才叫追上。七步还没追上，就记一次没追上。

Press “break three steps,” then “one step at a time.” The first step on which both arrive is when the held picture matches the truth. Seven steps without one is counted as not caught up.

两路不一样快 · Two speeds

手指三步来一次。按“来一步”，脑电每步都可能到，手指只在第三步到。画面全新的，一段最多六步。

The finger sends every third step. Press “one step”: the brain recording may arrive each step, the finger only on every third. At most six steps of sixteen are fully new.

两只手 · Two hands

一间房里两只手。各动各的，谁没到冻谁。A 没到几回，B 的手都不动一下。

Two hands in one room. Each moves on its own arrivals. A can miss as often as it likes; B’s hand does not flinch.

这是交付，不是生成器 · This is the delivery, not the generator

拿融资去做视频世界模型，做的是生成器：数据、训练、机器人评估。这个页面是放在生成器前面的检查。检查能在一个文件里算完，是因为交出去的每个数都有证明。它不替代生成器。

A round spent on a video world model builds the generator: data, training, a robot evaluation. This page is the check in front of that generator. The check fits in a file because each number it returns has a proof. It does not replace the generator.

在同样的 2,000 张图上，补全后的路径撞了 1,439 次。封住第一次撞上的那一格再规划，614 条能到，786 条在后面的格子上再撞，39 条无路。所以一次追问修不完。页面上的走廊就是这个形状。

On the same 2,000 grids the filled path crashes 1,439 times. Blocking the first crash cell and planning again: 614 arrive, 786 crash on a later cell, 39 have no path. One question does not finish the repair. The corridor above has that shape.

继续追问，直到不再撞或者无路可走：561 张图一次都不用问，653 张问一次就结束，786 张要问多于一次。追问合计 2,814 次。有一张图要问 9 次。一次追问不是典型情况。会撞的图里，多数需要连续问下去。

Keep asking until the path arrives or no path remains: 561 grids need no question, 653 are finished by one, and 786 need more than one. The questions total 2,814. One grid needs 9. One question is not the typical case. Most grids that crash need the questions to continue.
