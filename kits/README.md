# Local picture and sound kits

These kits sit on this computer so a scene can be built. They are not in git. The open-world picture is not a shipped Unreal level.

这些套件在本机，用来开始做场景。它们不进 git。开放世界的画面还不是一个已经发布的虚幻关卡。

## Engine

Epic Games Launcher 20.3.3 is installed. The Unreal Editor itself is not.

虚幻启动器 20.3.3 已经装上。虚幻编辑器本体还没有。

Unreal Engine 4.27 is the last UE4 release. Epic's notes for that Mac build name macOS Big Sur, the Xcode of that era, and Metal 1.2, with preliminary Apple Silicon support under Rosetta. This machine is Apple Silicon on macOS 26. A full editor is also larger than the free space that should be spent here.

虚幻引擎 4.27 是 UE4 的最后一版。Epic 对那一版 Mac 的说明写的是 macOS Big Sur、同期的 Xcode 和 Metal 1.2，Apple Silicon 只是在 Rosetta 下的初步支持。这台机器是 Apple Silicon，系统是 macOS 26。完整编辑器也比这里该占用的剩余空间更大。

The legal next step is to open the launcher and sign in. An editor binary copied from somewhere else is not a kit.

合法的下一步是打开启动器并登录。从别处拷来的编辑器二进制不是套件。

## Models

Two CC0 nature packs are unpacked. Kenney Nature Kit includes FBX, OBJ, GLTF, DAE, and STL. Quaternius Ultimate Nature Pack (June 2019) includes FBX, OBJ, and Blender files.

两套 CC0 自然场景模型已经解压。Kenney Nature Kit 含 FBX、OBJ、GLTF、DAE、STL。Quaternius Ultimate Nature Pack（2019 年 6 月）含 FBX、OBJ 和 Blender 文件。

## Music

Four movements of Beethoven's Piano Sonata No. 15 in D major, Op. 28, played by Karine Gilanyan. The Internet Archive item carries a CC public-domain mark. This is a recording library for the scene, not a new piece of music.

贝多芬《D 大调第 15 钢琴奏鸣曲》作品 28 的四个乐章，演奏者 Karine Gilanyan。Internet Archive 上的条目带有 CC 公有领域标记。这是场景用的录音库，不是新写的曲子。
